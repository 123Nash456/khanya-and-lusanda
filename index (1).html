LET’S CELEBRATE KHANYA & LUSANDA TOGETHER!

A gathering of love, gratitude and prayer for the couple and their little girl.

See the programme ↓
Order of the day

Programme

About 1 h 25 min – 1 h 45 min
  1. 1

    Arrival & Settling In

    10 min

    Guests arrive, find their seats, and place any remaining contributions in the designated collection area. Everyone is welcome to mingle, chat, and order food and drinks.

  2. 2

    Welcome & Opening Prayer

    5 min

    The host welcomes everyone, briefly shares the purpose of the gathering, and expresses heartfelt gratitude to everyone who contributed, regardless of the amount. The gathering opens with a prayer for the couple and their little one.

  3. 3

    Food, Drinks & Fellowship

    30–40 min

    Guests enjoy their meals, refreshments, and one another’s company in a relaxed and welcoming atmosphere.

  4. 4

    Fun Activity

    10 min

    A light-hearted activity to bring everyone together, create laughter, and make the gathering memorable.

  5. 5

    Collection Handover

    15–20 min

    The couple is invited to the centre of the gathering. The host shares a few heartfelt words about the purpose of the collection before formally handing over the baby clothes, vouchers, monetary contributions, and other gifts. The couple is given an opportunity to share a few words of appreciation.

  6. 6

    Words of Encouragement & Prayer

    5–10 min

    Guests share words of love, encouragement, and well wishes for the couple and their little one. A closing prayer is offered for their family, health, happiness, and journey ahead.

  7. 7

    Closing & Appreciation

    10 min

    The host thanks everyone for their presence, generosity, love, and support. Guests are invited to enjoy any remaining refreshments, take photos, and say their goodbyes.

♥ Thank you for being part of this special day.
